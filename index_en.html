01 / ABOUT

Rigorous learning, deliberately extended.

I am Chun-Hao Wen, a Mathematics student at National Taiwan Normal University, exploring machine learning questions through mathematical structure.

Algebra and linear algebra developed my sensitivity to abstract structure. Independent study in Galois theory, functional analysis, and machine learning has taught me to plan deliberately and commit over the long term.

Two visits to Singapore, in 2018 and 2025, clarified my aspiration for a high-standard academic environment built around growth. It led me to expand from pure mathematics into data science and ML: beginning with Graph Laplacians, GCNs, and optimization problems, I keep translating rigorous theory into research that can be examined and improved.

Goal: to continue deepening my work in a graduate programme in machine learning, data science, or AI.
